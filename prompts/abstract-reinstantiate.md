## Work through these steps in order

1. **Abstract the problem.** Restate it with no domain vocabulary: what quantity is being optimised or constrained, what is the contradiction or trade-off, what is conserved, what is the bottleneck, what has already been tried and why it fails. 5–10 lines.
2. **Retrieve from the seed.** List 5–7 concrete mechanisms, techniques or phenomena from {seed_name} — each one a named thing with a one-line description of how it works. Do *not* look at the problem while doing this; describe the seed on its own terms.
3. **Map.** For each mechanism, state whether it addresses the abstract problem from step 1, and if so build an explicit correspondence table: element in the seed ↔ element in the problem. Discard mechanisms where the only correspondence is a shared word or a vague image.
4. **Re-instantiate.** Take the 1–2 surviving mappings and write them out as a concrete method for the original problem: the steps, the quantities involved, and if applicable the maths or algorithm, adapted to the problem's actual objects.
5. **Critique.** Say plainly what is lost in translation, what assumption of the seed domain does not hold here, and what the cheapest experiment is that would tell us whether the transfer works.

Be specific. A proposal the home field could already have made without the seed is a failure.
